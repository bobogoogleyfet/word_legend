# a tile. A superword board is laid out along the word, so every letter on it
# belongs to the word. One round in ten is built around one of these.

# Curated from the common word list: no British variant where the American one is
# here, and one form per word (CIRCUMNAVIGATION, not also CIRCUMNAVIGATING), so
# the rotation does not serve the same word twice in all but name.
