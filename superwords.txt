#
# Each fills a board on its own: sixteen tiles, one letter each, with "qu" sharing
# a tile. A superword board is laid out along the word, so every letter on it
# belongs to the word. Every fifth round is built around one of these.
#
# Each line is the word and the theme shown under a board built around it.
#
# Curated for words players will recognise: no British variant where the
# American one is here, one form per word (CIRCUMNAVIGATION, not also
# CIRCUMNAVIGATING), and nothing crude.
